## What this repository contains

- Original source code: a PowerPC-to-Rust translator, a native runtime that stands in for the
  Xbox 360's kernel, GPU and audio hardware, a database reader and editor, and tools.
- Documentation of how the game's file formats and internal data structures work, written for
  interoperability, so that people can keep playing and modding a game they own.

## What it does not contain, and never will

- No game code: no `default.xex`, no recompiled output of it (`crates/game-blocks/parts/` is
  generated on your machine and git-ignored).
- No game assets: no disc images, textures, models, audio, fonts, databases or save files.
- No keys, no copy-protection circumvention, and no way to obtain the game.

## Using it

You need your own legally obtained copy of LMA Manager 2007 for the Xbox 360. Everything the
port runs is produced locally from your own files. Don't share the recompiled code, extracted game
files or builds that contain them.

## Contributions and issues

Never attach game files, extracted assets, recompiled code, save states or screenshots of
copyrighted data dumps to issues or pull requests. They will be removed.

If you are a rights holder with a concern about this project, please open an issue titled
"Rights holder contact" (no details needed in public) and the maintainer will reach out.
